Contact

Questions, bugs, ideas, or a post on the Community Wall to report? Open an issue on the Belovings support page. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information, photos, or credentials.

Common questions

My items did not sync. Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that Belovings is turned on under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps. Syncing needs an internet connection and can take a moment.

How do I share my library with my household? Open "My Belovings" at the top of the map and use the library settings to share it by iCloud. Everyone you invite sees and edits the same library.

What is a halo? A halo is a way to say goodbye to an item you no longer have. The item leaves your living library and moves to the Halos screen, where you can keep its photo and poem.

What is the Community Wall? When you halo an item you can choose to share its photo, name and poem on a public wall. Sharing is optional and on by default in the halo sheet, so switch it off if you want to keep a halo private. You can take your post down at any time from the Halos screen.

How do I report or hide a post? On the Halos screen, tap the ••• button on someone else's post to report it or hide it, or to hide everything from that person. Hiding is stored on your device only.

The Community Wall is empty or will not load. The wall needs an internet connection and an iCloud account signed in on the device.

How do I delete my data? Delete items and folders in the app to remove them from your iCloud library, and take down any Community Wall posts from the Halos screen. To have a post removed for you, contact us above.

Community rules

Posts must not contain hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal content, other people's private information, or anything you do not have the right to share. Reported posts are reviewed, and we remove those that break the rules and block abusive users.
