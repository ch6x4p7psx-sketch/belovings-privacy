Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

PrivacyCommunity WallSupport

This Privacy Policy explains how Belovings ("Belovings," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Belovings app. Belovings is a home inventory for the things you love. Your library is yours, and we do not run a server that collects it.

Your library stays in your iCloud

  • What is stored. Your folders, item names, photos, poems, colors, dimensions and tags are saved on your device and synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. If you share your library with household members, Apple's iCloud sharing delivers it to them.
  • We cannot read it. This data lives in your private iCloud storage. The developer has no access to it, and it is not sent to any server we operate.
  • Photos and capture. When you add an item, the camera (and LiDAR, where available) is used to capture it. Cutting out the item and choosing its colors happens on your device. Photos you import come from the photo library only with your permission.
  • On-device intelligence. Features that suggest names or poems use Apple's on-device capabilities where available.

The Community Wall (optional)

When you say goodbye to an item with a halo, you may choose to post it on the Community Wall, where other Belovings users can see it. Posting is a choice you make each time, and you pick what to include.

  • What is posted. Only what you choose: the item's photo, its name, and its poem, plus the date it was haloed. Nothing else about your library is shared.
  • Where it is stored. Posts are stored in the public CloudKit database for the app, which is operated by Apple. Each post is tied to an anonymous, app-specific CloudKit identifier, not to your name, email or location.
  • Removing a post. You can take your post off the wall at any time from the Halos screen, which deletes the public record.
  • Please don't post anything private. Anything you post is visible to other users. Avoid personal information, faces, addresses or documents in photos and text.

Reporting and hiding

Other people's posts can be reported from the Halos screen. A report stores the post's identifier and an anonymous reporter identifier in the public CloudKit database so we can review it. We remove posts that break the rules below. Hiding a post, or all posts from one person, is stored only on your device.

Community rules

Posts must not contain hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal content, other people's private information, or anything you do not have the right to share. We may remove any post and block abusive users from the wall. Reported posts are reviewed, and we aim to act on them within 24 hours.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in. Belovings has no registration.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • No use of your physical location.

We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Your library remains in your iCloud until you delete it; deleting items or folders in the app removes them from your iCloud copy, and you can manage or erase iCloud app data in iOS Settings. Community Wall posts remain until you take them off the wall or we remove them. To ask us to delete a post or a report you made, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Belovings is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date.

Support and contact

For help, questions, privacy requests, or to report content on the Community Wall, open an issue on the Belovings support page. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information, photos or credentials.

  • My items did not sync. Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on for Belovings in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps.
  • I can't see the Community Wall. The wall needs an internet connection and an iCloud account signed in on the device.
